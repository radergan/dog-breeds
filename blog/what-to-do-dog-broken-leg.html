What to Do When Your Dog Breaks Their Leg: A Guide from Experience

📅 2026-05-19 ✍️ Admin

My beagle Henry broke his leg jumping off the couch. His yelps were unlike anything I'd heard before, and I panicked. Here's what I learned:

3 Critical Steps

1. Stay Calm & Keep Them Still

Do not let your dog walk on the injured leg. Sit with them, speak softly, and prevent movement. Your panic makes them more anxious.

2. Stabilize (If Possible)

Use something rigid (magazine, ruler) alongside the leg and secure gently with cloth. Skip this if your dog is too distressed—transport is more important.

3. Emergency Vet NOW

Call ahead. Transport in a carrier (small dogs) or use a blanket as a stretcher (large dogs). Support the injured leg without moving it.

Henry's Recovery

Clean break in his tibia. Surgery with a pin, six weeks crate rest, $2,500 total. Full recovery—he's back to jumping everywhere.

Bottom line: Stay calm, keep them still, get to the vet. Act fast.
